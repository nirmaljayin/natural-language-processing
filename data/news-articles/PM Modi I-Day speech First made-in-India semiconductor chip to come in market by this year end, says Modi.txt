PM Modi I-Day speech: First made-in-India semiconductor chip to come in market by this year end, says Modi

The Prime Minister said that six semiconductor units are already on ground and four new units have been given green signal

First made-in-India semiconductor chip will be launched in the market by the end of this year, Prime Minister Narendra Modi said on Friday (August 15, 2025).

Addressing the nation on the 79th Independence Day, Mr. Modi said that six semiconductor units are already on ground and four new units have been given green signal.
"By the end of this year, made in India, made by the people of India, made in India chips will come to the market," Mr. Modi said.

He said that the country is moving towards becoming a global hub for semiconductor manufacturing. "We are working to make India a global hub for semiconductor manufacturing. The first made-in-India chip will be launched in the market by the end of this year," he added.
