How Trump's drive to his golf course shaped homeless crackdown

President Donald Trump says his administration will begin removing homeless people from the streets of Washington DC.

The announcement came a day after he posted a photo of tents by the side of the road on the way to his golf course - saying that "the homeless have to move out".

BBC Verify's Jake Horton has tracked the president's journey and mapped where the tents were which he highlighted as part of his "beautification" plan for the nation's capital.
